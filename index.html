Six coasts where Indian surfers paddle out. Operators and conditions change, so confirm before you travel.

Karnataka

Mulki

A well-known surf town near Mangaluru where the river meets the sea, with a regular local scene and lessons in season.

Tamil Nadu

South of Chennai, with a surf school and mellow waves that suit learners.

Kerala

Cliff-backed beaches where lessons are offered when the sea is gentle.

Find What You Need

Pick the right board for your level.

Soft-top foamie

Wide, thick and forgiving. It floats well and is soft if it hits you.

Mini-mal or funboard

A step up once you can stand on whitewater and want to catch green waves.

Longboard

Easy paddling and smooth rides on small, mellow waves.

Shortboard

Responsive but unstable. Leave it for later, when your balance is steady.

Quality You Can Trust

Safety and etiquette for the line-up.

  • Know the rip current rule

    If you are pulled out, do not fight it. Swim parallel to the shore until you are free, then come in.

  • Always wear a leash

    It stops your board becoming a hazard for others if you fall.

  • Respect priority

    The surfer closest to the breaking part of the wave has right of way. Never drop in on someone already riding.

  • Know your limits

    If waves are bigger than you can handle or you are tired, wait on the beach. There will be another day.

Simple & Convenient

Your first sessions, in order.

  1. Learn to paddle

    Lie in the centre of the board with your chest up and paddle with long strokes.

  2. Practise the pop-up on sand

    Hands by your chest, push up, bring your back foot under you, then step the front foot forward.

  3. Ride whitewater

    Stand waist-deep, face the beach, paddle and stand when you feel the push.

  4. Move to green waves

    Once standing feels easy, paddle past the breaking waves and pick small, clean ones.

Basic kit for a beginner.

  • Soft-top board

    A length of 7 to 8 feet suits many adult beginners.

  • Leash

    Sized to your board, with the cuff on your back ankle.

  • Rash vest

    Stops chafing and protects you from the sun.

  • Board shorts

    Choose a tie waist and a length that will not snag.

  • Wax or traction pad

    Gives your feet grip on the deck.

  • Mineral sunscreen

    Apply before you go in and reapply after the session.

Questions people ask

How long does it take to learn to surf?

Many beginners stand on whitewater within a few lessons. Riding unbroken waves takes weeks or months of regular practice.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

You need to be comfortable in the sea. Learn with an instructor who can keep an eye on you.

When is the best season to surf in India?

It varies by coast and by swell. Ask a local school for the season that suits your level.

What should I wear?

A rash vest and board shorts are enough in warm water. Add sun protection for your face and neck.
